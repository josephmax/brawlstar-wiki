{{Quote|Vince tends to both the espresso machine and his many-legged friends, much to his customers' dismay. He walks slowly around his Creepy Café, careful not to step on any of his beloved bugs. His companions, Mocha and Doppio, follow him everywhere.}}{{Portrait}}
Vince is a {{Rarity|Mythic}} [[Brawler]], who has moderate health, a long attack range, immense damage potential, and decent reload speed. He will be the 108th Brawler added to the game. His Trait causes collectible caterpillars to spawn in the area which can drastically alter his Super. His main attack fires a cup of coffee that deals moderate damage to a single target. His Super debuffs enemies and causes them to take increased damage depending on the amount of moths in his collection. Vince's first Gadget, Hot Coffee, allows him to toss a projectile in a considerable radius that deals area damage. His second Gadget, Metamothosis, lets Vince turn nearby caterpillars into moths while doubling the amount he would normally receive. His first Star Power, Come to Papa, causes otherwise stationary caterpillars to slowly crawl towards Vince, easing collection. His second Star Power, Bug Bandage, heals Vince for 1200 health for each moth he collects.

### Trait

{{Quote|Caterpillars periodically spawn near Vince. Hitting them or walking over them turns them into moths for his collection. Vince loses 50% of his moth when defeated (remaining moth are rounded down).}}{{Trait|Caterpillar}}
A caterpillar, up to five, will spawn near Vince every few seconds. The spawn pattern is cyclical; a caterpillar spawns 4 seconds in, then 5 seconds, and finally 6. The cycle then repeats. When Vince hits them with a coffee attack or walks over, they turn into a moth in his collection. Vince loses 50% of his collection when defeated.

### Attack: Espresso Shot

{{Quote|Vince serves up a fresh cup of coffee, dealing damage.}}
Vince fires a cup of coffee that deals moderate damage to enemies. The attack can also be fired at caterpillars to turn them into moths.

### Super: Barista Barrage

Vince's Super functions differently based on the amount of moths in Vince's collection, gaining a gimmick on top of previous ones for each of the Super's 4 possible stages. The main idea is that he fires one or multiple projectiles that debuffs any enemy hit, causing them to receive more damage from his main attacks. His Super first upgrades at 5 moths, then 10, and finally 20.

### Super: Barista Barrage / Barista Barrage

{{Quote|Shoots a single projectile, inflicting a caterpillar debuff. Main Attacks deal bonus damage when hitting an opponent with a Caterpillar debuff.}}
Vince fires a projectile around the width of [[Gus]]' main attack. If hit, it deals damage (plus 5% per moth) and debuffs the enemy for a few seconds, who will take 100 extra damage per moth from Vince's main attack. The projectile pierces brawlers and environment alike.

### Super: Barista Barrage / Barista Barrage 2

{{Quote|With 5+ moths in Vince's collection, his Super shoots 3 projectiles, inflicting a caterpillar debuff. Additionally, it reloads 1 ammo for each Brawler hit.}}
At 5+ moths, Vince fires two additional projectiles with a slight spread, similar to [[Clancy]]'s Stage 3 attack. These projectiles cause the same effect but also reload 1 ammo from enemy brawlers it hit.

### Super: Barista Barrage / Barista Barrage 3

{{Quote|With 10+ moths in Vince's collection, his Super shoots 5 projectiles, inflicting a caterpillar debuff. Additionally, it reloads 1 ammo for each Brawler hit and bounces to nearby enemies.}}
At 10+ moths, Vince fires another 2 extra projectiles in a larger spread. Similarly to Barista Barrage 2, they steal an ammo from affected enemies, but now also bounces towards nearby enemies on hit, causing the same effect.

### Super: Barista Barrage / Barista Barrage 4

{{Quote|With 20+ moths in Vince's collection, his Super shoots 7 projectiles, inflicting a caterpillar debuff. Additionlly, it reloads 1 ammo for each Brawler hit, bounces to nearby enemies, and slows them.}}
Finally, at 20+ moths, Vince fires 2 more projectiles. This time, however, the overall spread stays the same and the projectiles simply become more densely packed. On impact, the projectiles do everything it does in its previous stage, but also slow affected enemies.

### Gadgets / Hot Coffee

{{Quote|Vince throws an explosive cappuccino, dealing area damage. Cooldown: 15 seconds.}}{{Gadget|1}}
Vince throws an aimable cappuccino, which deals area damage equal to one of his main attacks without any moths.

### Gadgets / Metamothosis

{{Quote|Vince turns all nearby caterpillars into moths, gaining an additional moth for each collected. Cooldown: 13 seconds.}}
{{Gadget|2}}
Vince collects all caterpillars within a large radius and gains 2 moths (instead of 1) for each.

### Star Powers / Come to Papa

{{Quote|Nearby caterpillars crawl towards Vince.}}{{StarPower|1}}
Instead of being stationary, spawned caterpillars will slowly move towards Vince.

### Star Powers / Bug Bandage

{{Quote|Turning a caterpillar into a moth heals Vince for 1200.}}{{StarPower|2}}
Collecting a caterpillar heals Vince for 17.65% of his health. This also works with caterpillars collected by Metamothosis.
