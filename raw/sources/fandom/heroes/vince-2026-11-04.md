{{Quote|Vince tends to both the espresso machine and his many-legged friends, much to his customers' dismay. He walks slowly around his Creepy Café, careful not to step on any of his beloved bugs. His companions, Mocha and Doppio, follow him everywhere.}}{{Portrait}}
Vince is a {{Rarity|Mythic}} [[Brawler]], who has moderate health, a long attack range, an extreme damage potential, immense utility with his Super, and a fast reload speed. He is the 108th Brawler added to the game. His Trait causes collectible caterpillars to spawn in the area which strengthen his Super. His main attack fires a cup of coffee that deals moderate damage to a single target. His Super debuffs enemies and causes them to take increased damage from his main attack depending on the amount of moths in his collection. Vince's first Gadget, Hot Coffee, allows him to toss a projectile in a considerable radius that deals area damage. His second Gadget, Metamothosis, lets Vince turn nearby caterpillars into moths while doubling the amount he would normally receive. His first Star Power, Come to Papa, causes otherwise stationary caterpillars to slowly crawl towards Vince, easing collection. His second Star Power, Bug Bandage, heals Vince for each moth he collects. His Hypercharge, Bug Swarm, makes his Super spawn 6 Hypercharged around him that chase down the nearest enemies to damage them.

### Trait

{{Quote|Caterpillars periodically spawn near Vince. Hitting them or walking over them turns them into moths for his collection. Vince loses 50% of his moth when defeated (remaining moth are rounded down).}}{{Trait|Caterpillar}}
A caterpillar, up to five, will spawn near Vince every few seconds. The spawn pattern is cyclical; a caterpillar spawns 4 seconds in, then 5 seconds, and finally 6. The cycle then repeats. When Vince hits them with a coffee attack or walks over, they turn into a moth in his collection. Vince loses 50% of his collection when defeated, rounded down if an odd number. There is no limit on the number of caterpillars that can be collected. Caterpillars are also specific to each Vince in the game, so collecting teammates' or enemies' caterpillars is not possible. Caterpillars don't despawn nor be destroyed, and all projectiles will phase through. Caterpillars stop spawning once Vince collects 99 of them.

### Attack: Espresso Shot

{{Quote|Vince serves up a fresh cup of coffee, dealing damage.}}
Vince fires a cup of coffee that deals moderate damage to enemies. The attack can also be fired at caterpillars to turn them into moths.

### Super: Barista Barrage

Vince's Super functions differently based on the amount of moths in Vince's collection, gaining a gimmick on top of previous ones for each of the Super's 4 possible stages. The main idea is that he fires one or multiple projectiles that debuffs any enemy hit, causing them to receive more damage from his main attacks. His Super first upgrades at 5 moths, then at 10, and finally at 20.

### Super: Barista Barrage / Barista Barrage

{{Quote|Shoots a single projectile, inflicting a caterpillar debuff. Main Attacks deal bonus damage when hitting an opponent with a Caterpillar debuff.}}
Vince fires a projectile around the width of [[Gus]]' main attack. If hit, it deals damage (plus 5% per moth) and debuffs the enemy for 10 seconds, who will take 4.5% extra damage per moth from Vince's main attack. The projectile pierces brawlers and environment alike.

### Super: Barista Barrage / Barista Barrage 2

{{Quote|With 5+ moths in Vince's collection, his Super shoots 3 projectiles, inflicting a caterpillar debuff. Additionally, it reloads 1 ammo for each Brawler hit.}}
At 5+ moths, Vince fires two additional projectiles with a slight spread, similar to [[Clancy]]'s Stage 3 attack. These projectiles cause the same effect, but Vince now reloads 1 ammo for every enemy hit. Only 1 projectile per Super can deal damage to an enemy.

### Super: Barista Barrage / Barista Barrage 3

{{Quote|With 10+ moths in Vince's collection, his Super shoots 5 projectiles, inflicting a caterpillar debuff. Additionally, it reloads 1 ammo for each Brawler hit and bounces to nearby enemies.}}
At 10+ moths, Vince fires another 2 extra projectiles in a larger spread. Similarly to Barista Barrage 2, they reload Vince's ammo when hitting enemies, but they now also bounce to up to 1 nearby enemy on hit, causing the same effect.

### Super: Barista Barrage / Barista Barrage 4

{{Quote|With 20+ moths in Vince's collection, his Super shoots 7 projectiles, inflicting a caterpillar debuff. Additionlly, it reloads 1 ammo for each Brawler hit, bounces to nearby enemies, and slows them.}}
Finally, at 20+ moths, Vince fires 2 more projectiles. This time, however, the overall spread stays the same and the projectiles simply become more densely packed. On impact, the projectiles do everything it does in its previous stage, but also slow affected enemies.

### Gadgets / Hot Coffee

{{Quote|Vince throws an explosive cappuccino, dealing area damage. Cooldown: 15 seconds.}}{{Gadget|1}}
Vince throws an aimable cappuccino over walls, which deals area damage equal to one of his main attacks without any moths but is still affected by his Super's caterpillar debuff. It can also be thrown one tile further than his main attack. While not mentioned in its description, using this Gadget does consume 1 ammo. Caterpillar(s) can be collected if hit by the cappuccino, as if they were hit by a main attack.

### Star Powers / Bug Bandage

### Hypercharge: Bug Swarm

{{Quote|Vince's Super is always at max power and releases 6 Hypercharged caterpillars around him.}}{{Hypercharge}}
When activated, Vince's Super is at Barrista Barrage 4, and releases 6 Hypercharged caterpillars around him that follow and attack the closest enemies. The Hypercharged caterpillars have 2200 health, deal 500 damage each, and decay by 10% of their health per second until they're destroyed, lasting up to 10 seconds if they don't take any damage. Once the caterpillars are done, they each into moths in his collection. Vince also gains a 20% speed boost, as well as a 5% damage and shield boost.

### Tips / Game Modes and Maps

* Vince excels in modes where he's given time to collect his caterpillars and is faced with little threat from other Brawlers, making him a powerhouse in both [[Knockout]] and [[Wipeout]], where both teams tend to play defensively and allow Vince to stockpile power and dominate the endgame.
** In contrast, Vince is weak in high-intensity game modes such as [[Brawl Ball]] or [[Brawl Hockey]], where his gimmick prevents him from being at his full potential for most of the game and often puts him in skirmishes that he can't win.
* Vince's Super has an exceptionally wide range when fully upgraded, making him quite valuable for crowd control in 5V5 game modes. 
* While he is given plenty of time to collect caterpillars in [[Brawl Arena]], Vince must be careful not to get ambushed without his Super in Brawl Arena, as his base damage scaling is rather poor. He is excellent for taking care of bot swarms or finishing off aggressive enemies with his Super.
** Since his fully upgraded Super can bounce off of bots, which act similarly to Brawlers, Vince can use this to bounce multiple shots from a bot horde into a single Brawler, allowing him to deal monstrous amounts of damage.
** With a defensive team or even just a teammate that offers extra survivability (such as [[Poco]] or [[Doug]]), Vince can accumulate upwards of 40 caterpillars in a single game, allowing him to out-damage the healing of more resilient Brawlers used in the game mode, such as Doug or [[Kenji]].
* Vince's usefulness in [[Hot Zone]] depends on the map; more open maps such as Ring of Fire let him gather caterpillars and fight back more easily, while more closed maps like Parallel Plays leave him vulnerable to aggressive Brawlers and make gathering caterpillars more difficult.
* Vince is very capable of assassinating gem carriers in [[Gem Grab]] and is a powerful defensive Brawler, making him good at supporting his team's gem carrier and stopping aggressive Brawlers diving for the gems. Owing to his poor ability to protect himself, he is a poor pick for gem carrier.
* As his Super doesn't work on the Safe, Vince is a terrible pick in [[Heist]] as while capable of defending his own Safe, he is unable to deal substantial damage.
* Camping in [[Showdown]] may not work effectively even without the Come to Papa Star Power because the presence of a caterpillar in the vicinity can warn other players that Vince is nearby (since they spawn near Vince). The caterpillars may also die in the poison smoke, making collection harder at later stages.

### Tips / Recommended Build

* It is imperative that Vince stay on the field for as long as possible, so as to allow him to accumulate as many caterpillars as possible. As such, the Shield Gear is a good option for protecting him from enemy attacks and allowing him to explore more safely.
* Since he will spend a good portion of his time gathering caterpillars, Vince will often be out of combat and needs to heal to get back into the action. As such, the Healing Gear is useful as he can quickly recuperate and assist his teammates.
* While the Damage gear can boost his damage enough that he can take down most Brawlers quickly with his Super-enhanced attack, he is too frail to regularly be in a comfortable position to do this, making this gear a niche pick.
* Come To Papa doesn't make the caterpillars move very fast, doesn't offer much needed survivability and also betrays Vince's location to enemies, making it the less ideal Star Power. It can be decent on very open maps, however, where Vince may be too busy dodging to go to their location and prevent his movement from being too predictable for enemies.
* Hot Coffee not only extends the range of Vince's attack but also makes it deal AOE damage, making it perfect for finishing off marked mobs or taking down opponents hiding behind walls. It is best used in modes and maps wherein opponents tend to come in large mobs, such as Brawl Arena or maps with choke points, and can be used to surprise opponents that are escaping.
** It also doesn't count as one of Vince's ammo, meaning that he can use Hot Coffee as a fourth attack, which when combined with his Super boost is often enough to finish off most Brawlers.
* Metamothosis should be used in a pinch when Vince is being attacked but has his Super and is in an area with many caterpillars, as it will immediately buff his boosted damage and let him take out the enemy quickly. Alternatively, it is good for collecting all caterpillars nearby quickly if they're hard to access or find, and can buff his damage to change the tide.
** Additionally, with his Bug Bandage Star Power, he can use this Gadget as a source of quick healing if there are many caterpillars nearby.
* As Vince is rather frail and often exposes himself to danger searching for caterpillars, his Bug Bandage Star Power allows him to heal constantly while getting more of them.

### Tips / Strategies

* Despite his immense damage potential, Vince is very weak by himself and as such, should play more passively than most Damage Dealers, collecting caterpillars, staying alive and getting occasional shots in to charge his Super. Even then he should try to use his Super to support teammates and dissuade an enemy charge.
** Since Vince is quite frail and his attack can slightly clip through walls, one should strafe backwards while firing to charge up a Super and escape damage.
* Vince's Super and damage are mediocre below 10 caterpillars, so play passively until you get more than 10, after which his damage and area control capabilities are raised greatly.
* Since his Super pierces walls, Vince can team wipe with a caterpillar count of 20+ by hiding behind a wall near a chokepoint, launching his Super and then emerging to attack them.
* Always keep your Super at the ready, as if ambushed by another Brawler it can make the difference between winning or getting defeated.
* Ammo conservation is very important with Vince, as his ammo is used both for offence and collecting caterpillars, forcing him to conserve it carefully. As such, try to position yourself so that you can collect multiple caterpillars with a single shot, or even hit Brawlers with a shot meant for collection.
* If fired right next to one of his caterpillars, Vince can actually make his attack pierce. This makes him able to fight off crowds when nearby caterpillars, though use cases of this are very niche.
* After collecting twenty caterpillars, de-prioritise caterpillar collection since maximum Super power is achieved at twenty caterpillars. However collecting caterpillars may still be beneficial as the additional damage dealt scales with the number of caterpillars collected and can provide a cushion to avoid losing the Super upgrade once defeated.

### Tips / Other

* On bushy maps, Vince's caterpillars may be hard to see. As such, try to remember roughly where they spawned, which is indicated by a green splash, and watch out for their heads peeking out of the grass from time to time.
* While Vince's caterpillars cannot be damaged by Brawlers, they are still counted as summons and are instantly defeated by neutral sources of damage, such as the poison smoke. Take note of this in modes or maps where such threats are present, such as Showdown or Knockout.
** Additionally, they are capable of activating Brawler-activated entities such as teleporters, launch pads, and [[Bo]]'s mines, as well as uncloaking invisible opponents near them, so take note of this when fighting against Vince or using him against such Brawlers.
* Unlike other stockpile Brawlers, Vince can increase his power without engaging in fights, meaning that he is dangerous to ignore. Try to take him out as soon as possible, preventing him from upgrading his Super early.
* Vince lacks any defensive capabilities whatsoever and is very weak to aggressive Brawlers, so stick near Brawlers with crowd control abilities and play defensively to limit advances. Keep his Super in reserve in case of any assassination attempts so he can finish them off quickly.
* Vince's Super mark cannot bounce from summons and is absorbed by them, so try not to attack Brawlers hiding behind them.
* Vince's caterpillars are visible to everyone on the map, so it's often a safe bet to camp out nearby them to ambush a Vince.
* As the hitboxes of his caterpillars are very small when compared to other collecting Brawlers like [[Pierce]] and spawn rather far away, Vince should prioritise using his ammo to collect them instead of walking over, as the latter method is quite time consuming and can make you give up good positioning.
