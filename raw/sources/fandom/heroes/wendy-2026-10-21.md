Wendy is a {{Rarity|Mythic}} [[Brawler]] who has the second-lowest health and a moderate damage output, but has a fast movement and reload speed, a long range, and immense survivability thanks to her attack, Super, and Trait. She was the 106th Brawler added to the game. Her Traits allow her to move over water, and to charge her Super from enemies damaging her shield. She attacks by firing a blast that damages enemies, shields allies, and provides herself a shield that absorbs damage. Her Super is a wind turbine that passively reduces some damage Wendy and her allies take, redirecting that same amount of damage done to the turbine instead. Her first Gadget, Wind-Powered, makes Wendy jump forward. Her second Gadget, Green Grenade, makes her lob a green grenade that temporarily slows enemies down and prevents them from healing. Her first Star Power, Slowing Shield, slows enemies down while inside Wendy's Super. Her second Star Power, Solar Shield, increases the wind turbine's health. Her Hypercharge, Green Energy, makes her Super have more health and absorb more damage.

### Traits / Water

{{Quote|This Brawler can move over water.}}{{Trait|Water}}
Wendy's first Trait allows her to be able to move on water.

### Traits / Shield Damage

{{Quote|Charges Super when taking shield damage.}}{{Trait|Shield Damage}}
Wendy's other Trait allows her to charge her Super whenever she takes shield damage from enemy Brawlers or spawnables. The Super charge rate depends on the damage the enemy deals. It takes 6.67x of the shield's max health of damage to charge her Super completely, and all damage is calculated after shielding and other effects. Wendy also spawns with a shield already active, which is equal to 40% of her maximum health. This shield does not decay, but can only be replenished with her main attack.

### Attack: Blow Dryer

{{Quote|Wendy fires a blast dealing 2000 damage and giving herself 600 shield. When hitting a teammate, Wendy gives them 1520 shield.}}[[File:Hardcore Icon.png|left|40x40px|link=]]
Wendy fires a blast that deals moderate damage to enemies, has a long range, and provides herself a consumable shield that is 30% of the damage. However, upon firing the attack on allies, she instead provides them a shield that is 30.4% of her max original health. Wendy's shield on herself does not decay, but her allies' decay for 5% health every 0.5 seconds, similar to other consumable shields. If an attack deals more damage than the shield's current health, the shield disappears and Wendy and her allies with the shield is damaged by the remaining damage. [[Colette]]'s attack and Super will deal damage based on the Brawler's max health when this shield is active. Enemies damaging those with the shield on can still charge their Supers, but allies with Traits allowing them to charge Super from taking damage cannot. The shield on allies does stack, up to 160% of Wendy's original max HP.

### Super: Planet Protector

{{Quote|Wendy deploys a shield generator that absorbs 60% of damage taken by Wendy and her teammates within its range.}}
Wendy lobs a wind turbine that covers a 6-tile radius. The turbine passively reduces all damage Wendy and her allies take by 60% by redirecting the damage to the Super instead.

### Gadgets / Wind-Powered

{{Quote|Wendy's platform gives her a boost and she jumps a short distance. Cooldown: 18 seconds.}}{{Gadget|1}}
Wendy jumps forward 5 tiles in the direction she's facing, similar to [[Brock]]'s Rocket Laces Gadget, allowing her to jump over walls or water. If required to cross over a larger amount of blocks or over lakes, her jumping distance will be increased up to a limit or to the nearest piece of ground. While in the air, Wendy is completely immune to all damage besides status effects and damage applied over time. The Gadget cannot be used at less than max range unless its forced to by obstacles.

### Gadgets / Green Grenade

{{Quote|Wendy launches a wind grenade that slows all enemies hit and stops them healing momentarily. Cooldown: 18 seconds.}}
{{Gadget|2}}
Wendy lobs a wind grenade that explodes in a 2-tile radius, slowing the enemies hit for 5 seconds and preventing them from healing for 7 seconds. It doesn't deal damage and consumes ammo.

### Star Powers / Solar Shield

### Hypercharge: Green Energy

{{Quote|Increased Shield Generator health and absorb 90% of damage taken by Wendy or her teammates.}}{{Hypercharge}}
When activated, Wendy's Planet Protector has 1000 more HP and absorbs 90% damage from attacks. She also gains a 20% speed boost, and a 5% damage and shield boost.

### Tips / Game Modes and Maps

*If you are playing [[Brawl Ball]], you can feed your attacks to the highest HP teammate (e.g. [[Frank]] with the Sponge Star Power), who can grab the ball and charge to the opponent team's goal. This is especially easy to execute if the opponents do not have Crowd Control abilities or if the teammate has Crowd Control immunity.
**If you put down your Super, the strategy is much more viable because your charging teammate essentially has 5000 extra HP as your Super will redirect 5000 damage the charging teammate would have received to itself. Either Star Power benefits the team by either making it difficult for opponents to defend the goal or making the charge easier to pull off.
**Your 3rd teammate can be a healer (e.g. [[Byron]] or [[Poco]]) so your charging teammate can heal and become even more resistant to damage.
**Alternatively, your 3rd teammate can be a fast moving Brawler (e.g. [[Mortis]] or [[Crow]]) who will ensure your team gets the ball and pass to the charging teammate.

### Tips / Other

*You can essentially treat Wendy's shield on herself as bonus health: it doesn't decrease automatically, and is used in place of the 5000 base health, although also doesn't increase automatically. Therefore, Wendy when she spawns should be considered as if she has 7000 HP.
