Poco is a {{Rarity|Rare}} [[Brawler]] who has moderately high health, a medium range, and the ability to provide immense healing to his teammates, but very low damage. He was one of the 15 Brawlers who released at the first Beta launch. For his attack, Poco plays his guitarrón, sending damaging musical notes within a wide spread and range that deals minimal damage and heal teammates for a small amount. His Super fires a larger and faster musical wave, covering a longer distance and healing himself and any teammates in a larger amount within its range. His first Gadget, Tuning Fork, gives out a circular musical wave around him that allows himself and teammates in it to heal over a few seconds. His second Gadget, Protective Tunes, cleanses teammates from any adverse status effects and protects teammates from those effects momentarily in an throwable area-of-effect. His first Star Power, Da Capo!, increases the amount of healing granted by his main attack. His second Star Power, Screeching Solo, allows his Super to deal moderately low damage to enemies it hits. His Hypercharge, Medic's Melody, makes his Super overheal teammates with a shield that decays over time.

### Attack: Power Chord

{{Quote|Poco strums his guitarrón, sending forward bone-jarring sound waves. Allies Hit by the waves are healed and enemies take damage.}}Poco releases a wave from his guitarrón that takes the appearance of musical notes. The attack has a medium range and spreads out drastically as it travels, allowing it to cover a very large area and pierce multiple enemies caught in it, but deals a very low amount of damage. Teammates hit by the attack will be healed for 5% of Poco's max health.

### Super: Encore

{{Quote|Poco heals himself and all friends he can reach with his uplifting melody. Does not affect enemies.}}When his Super is used, he shoots a larger musical wave from his guitarrón that heals him and any teammates hit by the wave a very high amount. The wave is much wider and longer than his attack, but has the same cone shape as his attack. This can travel through any Brawler or obstacle. Poco's Super will appear green regardless of his team. Poco's Super can't heal minions summoned by teammates (e.g, [[Mr. P]]'s porters, [[Nita]]'s bear, [[Tara]]'s shadow clones, or [[Larry & Lawrie|Lawrie]]).

### Gadgets / Tuning Fork

{{Quote|Poco and all nearby allies heal for 1400 health 3 times. Cooldown: 20 seconds.}}

Poco releases a musical wave with a 3.33-tile radius around him on Gadget activation, then 1 additional wave every 2 seconds for 4 seconds. Each wave heals Poco and teammates in the radius for 1400 health. This Gadget's cooldown starts 4 seconds after activation.

### Gadgets / Protective Tunes

{{Quote|Poco creates a large AoE lasting 4 seconds that removes all adverse effects and grants them immunity while inside. Cooldown: 24 seconds.}}
{{Gadget|2}}
Poco lobs a musical note that creates a large area-of-effect in a 3.33-tile radius. This removes poisons, burns, stuns, slows, [[Lou]]'s Frost, [[Belle]]'s Electro-Bolts, [[Cordelius]]' Poison Mushrooms Gadget and [[Otis]]' Silent Seabed cancelling effect, as well as others. It does not interrupt displacements such as knockbacks, launch pads, and [[El Primo]]'s Suplex Supplement Gadget. Teammates will be immune to these effects while in the area. This Gadget's cooldown starts after the button is pressed.

{{Quote|Protective Tunes' area is larger and lasts longer.}}{{Buffie|Gadget}}
With the Buffie, the radius is increased to 4 tiles and the duration is increased from 4 to 6 seconds.

### Star Powers / Screeching Solo

{{Quote|Poco's Super now also hits enemies, dealing 1520 damage.}}
{{StarPower|2}}
Poco's Super will deal equal to his main attack's damage to enemy Brawlers in range, in addition to healing teammates. This damage does not build any Super charge. Note that auto-aiming your Super still aims it towards teammates instead of enemies.

{{Quote|Enemies hit by his Super are silenced for 0.5 sec.}}{{Buffie|Star}}[[File:OtisSuperIcon.png|left|40x40px|link=]]
With the Buffie, Poco's Super also prevents affected enemies from using any attacks, Supers, Gadgets, or Hypercharges, similar to [[Otis]]' Super, [[Cordelius]]' second Gadget or [[Sandy]]'s Hypercharged Super, for 0.5 seconds. This doesn't affect passive effects like the [[Gears|Speed Gear]]'s speed boost or [[Bo]]'s Circling Eagle Star Power. This works on spawnables like [[Nita]]'s bear, [[Penny]]'s cannon, or [[Jessie]]'s turret; however, it won't affect passive turrets like [[8-Bit]]'s and [[Pam]]'s turret. For the whole duration, a red, crossed-out symbol will show above the head of the affected target, signifying that this effect is active, and if it hits an enemy Brawler, the affected Brawler's screen will turn a bit dark.

### Tips / Game Modes and Maps

*It is advised to use his Screeching Solo Star Power when playing [[Showdown]], [[Lone Star]], [[Takedown]], or other solo events, due to the lack of teammates to heal with Da Capo!. This Star Power is especially effective if Poco has his Super charged while standing with multiple low-health enemies. 
**However, Poco is never too viable in such Solo events as he lacks a sufficient damage output and a use for his support abilities. He also suffers due to these game modes maps being bigger and more open, making it harder for Poco to charge his Super effectively, and is an easy target for Tanks and Assassins due to his lack of personal defense.
*With most of Poco's abilities being revolved around longevity, he can be a valuable asset in [[Hot Zone]]. Not only is he able to heal his teamates with his Super, Tuning Fork Gadget, and Da Capo! Star Power, but the former two can also affect himself, which can make him a passable defender for a zone after factoring in his decently high health. Although his damage is low, it is able to handle multiple opponents at once, which is useful in single-zone maps.

### Tips / Recommended Build

*When using Poco's Da Capo! Star Power, try lining up his attacks so you can chain them to heal teammates and damage enemies simultaneously, similar to how you use [[Byron]]'s Injection Star Power. This is the best way to get the most value out of Da Capo!, and grants your teammates more survivability whilst chipping away at enemies. The same logic applies with his Screeching Solo Star Power.
*Even though Poco is a support Brawler, his Screeching Solo Star Power gives him hybrid capabilities, as this Star Power allows Poco to deal damage to enemy Brawlers and heal himself at the same time. Even so, Poco's Super with Screeching Solo should not be used to deal damage in the same manner as his attack. It's also important to note that Poco's Screeching Solo damage is fairly low, so it's usually not a great idea to use his Super to deal damage, and is best used to finish off low-health enemies instead of directly damaging enemies.
*When fighting Brawlers with slow, poison, stun, or other effects, such as [[Crow]], Frank, [[Bea]], [[Amber]], [[Lou]], Willow, Buzz, [[Kit]], [[Angelo]], Byron, and [[Ollie]] in control-based modes such as [[Gem Grab]] and [[Hot Zone]], use the Protective Tunes Gadget. It will allow Poco to disable their effects temporarily, both on himself and on teammates within range. This can be especially useful against Brawlers with poison effects like Crow, [[Byron]], Angelo, [[Willow]], and [[Glowy]] as it will render their attacks ineffective and disable their healing reduction, allowing Poco to save teammates and secure control of the map. This Gadget can also completely disable Kit, [[Buzz]], and [[Frank]]'s stun and [[Belle]]'s mark.

### Tips / Strategies

*Poco with other healers can be incredible at healing. Poco could constantly heal [[Berry]], [[Pam]], [[Doug]], and/or [[Byron]] and vice versa to stay alive almost indefinitely, or in 3v3 events, two such healers can continuously heal a third teammate while they attack enemies and attempt to gain control of the map. Beware of Crow or other Brawlers with anti-heal abilities that could cripple the team, or utilize the Protective Tunes Gadget to mitigate their effects.

### Tips / Other

*Poco is great at supporting teammates due to his ability to heal them using his Super, his Tuning Fork Gadget, and his Da Capo! Star Power. Prioritize healing over damaging enemies with Poco because Poco's healing can help teammates win many battles and interactions by keeping them alive.
*Remember, Poco's attack has a wide spread but doesn't deal much damage. This means he's not used as a front line attacker, but rather used for effective support. Positively, the spread allows him to hit multiple targets at once and charge his Super quickly. The wide spread of his attack also allows him to check large bushes.
*Poco's attacks can be released in quick succession, allowing him to attack an enemy Brawler multiple times to charge his Super quickly before retreating to reload again, or heal a teammate quickly and effectively with his Da Capo! Star Power.
*Poco's healing abilities are more useful than [[Pam]]'s in tight situations when you or your teammates are on low health, as Pam's Super takes time, while Poco's Super is instant and occurs in a much larger area. This makes his healing considerably more useful than that of Pam in offense and survival-oriented modes like [[Bounty]] where teammates cannot afford to stay near Pam's turret for long.
*Poco is amazing when paired up with tanks as Poco can continuously replenish their health while they keep Poco safe from stronger and aggressive Brawlers and control the map. Using Poco's Tuning Fork Gadget and Poco's Da Capo! Star Power will be more ideal in this case, since with them, Poco can heal teammates even without his Super.
*Poco's attack is divided into 4 rows of musical notes that don't come out simultaneously, with the side rows moving faster. Aiming his attack at the corner will gain Poco a small amount of additional range. This is especially useful when fighting enemies with greater range than him.
*Using the main attack and then immediately using the Super at the same time will allow Poco to charge up some of his next Super. This can be best exploited by using Screeching Solo, allowing Poco to surprise the enemy with sudden extra damage while healing teammates and recharging his Super faster.
*Don't be afraid to use your Super in situations where you need to heal yourself to stay alive, since Poco needs to stay close to his teammates to continue providing support, and his Super charge is extremely fast, requiring only 4 hits to fully charge, which is best justified by his wide attack range.
