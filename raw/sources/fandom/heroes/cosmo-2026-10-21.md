Cosmo is a {{Rarity|Mythic}} [[Brawler]] who has moderate health, a long range, a variable damage output, a slow reload speed, and immense utility with his Super. He was the 107th Brawler added to the game. Cosmo attacks by throwing 3 planets that orbit in a circle, growing in size and damage the further they travel. For his Super, he fires a projectile that, upon hitting an enemy target, temporarily summons a black hole, which allows his and his allies' projectiles to strongly home in on the targets. His first Gadget, Planetary Pushback, lobs a projectile that knocks enemies back. His second Gadget, Telescope Trap, lobs his telescope to temporarily reveal an area, including enemies, and the enemies inside it cannot use movement abilities. His first Star Power, Precession, increases the orbit speed of his attacks. His second Star Power, Ammo Attractor, reloads 2 ammo when Cosmo's Super hits enemies.

### Attack: Orbit

{{Quote|Cosmo fires 3 projectiles that orbit in a circle. The projectiles get further apart as they travel, while growing in size and damage.}}
Cosmo attacks by firing 3 planets that orbit in a circle. These planets grow in size and deal more damage the further they travel. The farther the planets travel, the more damage they will inflict, so Cosmo is less effective against enemies near him, but excels at supporting his team at range.

### Super: Gravitation Pull

{{Quote|Cosmo fires a positive magnet that attaches to an enemy. Teammate's projectiles then gain extreme homing towards the target.}} 
Cosmo fires a projectile that, upon hitting an enemy target, spawns a black hole that follows them for 6 seconds. If his or his allies' projectiles pass through that area, the projectiles will strongly home in on the targets (it cannot miss). The change also applies to thrown projectiles, like [[Dynamike]], [[Grom]], and [[Barley]]'s attack or [[Sprout]], [[Squeak]], and [[Brock]]'s Super. However, this doesn't apply to Brawlers who uses themself as a projectile, like [[Mortis]], [[Kenji]], and [[Mico]]'s attack or [[El Primo]], [[Fang]], [[Kit]], [[Bull]], [[Darryl]], and [[Surge]]'s Super.

### Tips / Game Modes and Maps

* In [[Knockout]] and [[Bounty]], Cosmo shines on long-range, open maps such as Shooting Star or Out in the Open. His expanding orbit main attack allows him to out-damage opposing Sharpshooters from maximum range without risking his lower health pool. Additionally, landing his Super on an enemy sniped behind cover allows long-range allies (such as [[Piper]], [[Brock]], [[Rico]], or [[Nani]]) to finish them off with homing shots, securing an instant player advantage.
* In [[Hot Zone]], Cosmo acts as a top-tier zone controller. Placing his Telescopic Trap Gadget over the zone prevents high-mobility Brawlers like [[Stu]], [[Melodie]], [[Max]], or [[Mortis]] from using dashes or jumps to contest the area. If aggressive Tanks try to capture the zone, firing his main attack into the center forces them to take massive outer-orbit damage or retreat.
* In [[Heist]], while Cosmo lacks high point-blank burst, his Super paired with high-volume or multi-bullet Brawlers (such as [[8-Bit]], [[Colt]], [[Lola]], or [[Clancy]]) creates devastating objective setups. Marking an enemy Brawler standing near the safe forces all incoming ally fire to home in directly through the target onto the safe, shredding both the Brawler and the objective simultaneously. However, avoid playing him on tight, heavy-wall Heist maps where close-range Tanks can jump onto you before you can setup your orbit range.
* In [[Brawl Ball]], Cosmo functions primarily as a lane-denial defensive anchor. He can block enemies from rushing the ball with his Planetary Pushback Gadget, which knocks back approaching threats and disrupts ball carriers. You can also use the Super on the enemy ball carrier to allow your teammates to auto-aim and eliminate them instantly before they can reach your goal line.

### Tips / Recommended Build

* In open or long-range maps, use Cosmo's Precession Star Power to increase the projectile speed of his orbiting planets, making it vastly easier to connect outer-arc shots on dodging enemies at maximum range.
* His Planetary Pushback Gadget is crucial when dealing with aggressive Tanks and Assassins; trigger it right before an enemy closes the distance to knock them back into your high-damage outer orbit.
* On objective-heavy modes like Hot Zone or against high-mobility Brawlers like Stu and Mortis, swap to the Telescopic Trap Gadget to lock down wide areas and prevent enemies from using dashes or jumps to push your lane.
* When charging your Super, save it for moments when your team is fully reloaded and positioned to fire, as marking a target with it forces all incoming ally projectiles to home in on them for a guaranteed elimination.

### Tips / Strategies

* Cosmo's Super creates a gravity field on a target, causing all nearby allied projectiles to curve directly into the marked Brawler for a brief duration. You can exploit this mechanic to hit enemies behind cover, as high-damage sharpshooters like Piper, Brock, [[Maisie]], or Bea can fire straight past a wall and have their shots bend automatically toward the marked target. 
* Additionally, applying to an enemy Brawler right as they activate a channelled or dash-based Super (such as [[Frank]], Brock, or [[Carl]]) allows your team to collapse on them instantaneously from long range before their ability can finish, completely turning the tide of a team fight without putting yourself in danger.

### Tips / Other

* Cosmo moves at a standard Normal movement speed, meaning you must rely heavily on distance and map geometry rather than raw speed to keep aggressive Brawlers away.
* His main attack projectiles travel in an expanding circular orbit around him, allowing him to hit enemies hiding behind small walls or pillars if you angle the curve of the outer arc around the cover. It is crucial to remember that Cosmo deals minimum damage at point-blank range and maximum damage at his outermost orbit. If an enemy Brawler gets too close to you, do not attempt to trade main attacks; instead, use terrain or your Planetary Pushback Gadget to quickly re-establish maximum range.
* Cosmo struggles to hit opponents directly in front of him at long range, so instead aim your attack with the edges facing your target so that the rotating planets can hit them.
* Cosmo's Super bypasses traditional line-of-sight restrictions because it marks a designated location or target directly. This allows you to apply pressure on snipers or low-health enemies hiding deep behind enemy lines without exposing yourself to danger. Additionally, when fighting near narrow choke points or water gaps, placing inside the tight corridor guarantees that any allied projectiles fired into the area will hit, completely blocking the enemy team's path.
