Deathcap Trap (previously called Deathcap Cave, originally Mushroom Cave) is an official map for the [[Gem Grab]] and [[Bounty]] events. This map has a Brawlidays version named Spruce Up.

Deathcap Trap is an identical replica of this map under the same name for use in the [[Bounty]] event.

==Layout==
The map has an open center with small bush patches in the center bottom and top of the map. The right and left side of the center bottom and top contain a Z-like bush that has access to the center. Each corner has another patch where Brawlers can trap themselves in if they would like to remain hidden from view. The map is diagonally symmetrical.

==Tips==
*[[Emz]], [[Poco]], and [[Frank]]'s cone-shaped attacks can cover the whole center, making it hard for the enemies to collect the Gems.
*[[Mr. P]], [[Penny]], [[Jessie]], and other similar Brawlers can place their Super in the Z-like bush on their side behind a horizontal wall for maximum efficiency.
*Tanks should aim to go to the left lane and enter the enemy's Z-like bush for protection.
**Examples of useful tanks include [[Jacky]] and [[Rosa]]. [[Bull]], [[Darryl]], [[El Primo]], and other heavyweights lack the utility that these Brawlers have that make them shine. 
*Preferably, Brawlers that are aiming to hold the center should have medium to long range. [[Pam]] and [[8-Bit]] can place their Super in the horizontal wall inside the Z-like bush to have access to it on both lanes.
*Brawlers that have medium-ranged attacks should be more aggressive and enter the right lane. The enemy's right lane is more open, however it has a wall clusters where Brawlers can hide in.
**Medium-ranged Brawlers that can take a more aggressive role include [[Carl]], [[Sandy]], [[Nita]], [[Frank]], and [[Tara]].
*Marksmen like [[Piper]], [[Colt]], [[Bea]], [[Mandy]], [[Rico]], [[Angelo]], [[Maisie]], or [[Brock]] can also do well on this map. Keep checking the enemy side's bushes to avoid getting ambushed.
