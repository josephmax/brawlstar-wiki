## Selected Wikitext Excerpts

### Lead

Quick Travel is a community map for the Hot Zone event.

### Layout

The map has two zones split by thinly lined rope fencing. Connecting the zones are S-like bush clusters stretching inwards towards launch pads. One of which launches Brawlers to the nearest zone while the other launches them behind walls in the opposite zone. The map is diagonally symmetrical.

### Tips

- Playing defensively is hard on this map. Brawlers should plan on pushing enemies back instead of cluttering in zones, especially in the two S-shaped bush clusters while keeping the enemies away from the Zones.
- Only take the launch pad when playing as a heavyweight or a high health Brawler. Taking it as a low health Brawler is a risky option.
- Emz's attack can reach through the rope fencing and cover a large area. This makes her good as a support Brawler and on defense. Her Super can also decently scan the bush clusters.
- Amber's Super provides substantial control over her enemies. In case of heavyweight opponents destroying bushes is also recommended.
- Gale's Spring Ejector Gadget allows for easy transport around the map. However, make sure to reposition them if an enemy is trying to use them.
- Rosa can use her Unfriendly Bushes Gadget to reveal and slow enemies in the bushes and ambush them.
