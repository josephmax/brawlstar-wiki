```wikitext
Rustic Arcade is a community map for the [[Gem Grab]] event.

==Layout==
The map's center is split into a hexagon with walls facing the center and bushes bordering the opposite side, leaving an empty space between them and the gem spawn. Towards the spawns are six enclaves with bushes and two launch pads that launch Brawlers to the center. The map is diagonally symmetrical.

==Tips==
*High-spread Brawlers work great at controlling the center and blocking entrances. However, they should be at least mid-ranged. [[Pam]], [[Emz]], [[Frank]], or [[Poco]], both of which make excellent healers, have both the spread and the range to satisfy this.
*Artillery will struggle with using the open space and should be avoided. This includes short-ranged tanks as well. Mid-range Brawlers will work as an alternative, but are recommended to be support Brawlers. At least one sharpshooter should suffice.
*Long-ranged single-projectile marksmen, preferably [[Brock]], [[Bea]], [[Belle]], [[Mandy]], [[Maisie]], or [[Piper]], can assist gem carriers in the side lanes or middle lane. Breaking down the six barriers should extend their range potential.
*[[Tara]], [[Bo]], [[Griff]], and [[Carl]] should act more aggressive on this map because they aren't restricted by the same counters they may have seen in [[Crystal Arcade]]. Staying just behind the center top walls is recommended. If enemies do retreat to the corner bushes, it will be easy to pinch them.
```
