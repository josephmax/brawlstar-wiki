===Format===
* '''Participants'''
** 5 teams from main regions qualify directly by BSC points
** 2 Chinese Mainland teams qualify through Championship Finals
** 1 team qualifies through Challengers Finals
** Slots are allocated as follows:
*** 2 for [[Brawl_Stars_Championship/2026/Chinese_Mainland/Finals|Chinese Mainland]]
*** 2 for [[Brawl_Stars_Championship/2026/Leaderboards/EMEA|EMEA]] (including one won during the [[Brawl_Stars_Championship/2026/Brawl_Cup|Brawl Cup]])
*** 1 for [[Brawl_Stars_Championship/2026/Leaderboards/North_America|North America]], [[Brawl_Stars_Championship/2026/Leaderboards/South_America|South America]] and [[Brawl_Stars_Championship/2026/Leaderboards/East Asia|East Asia]]
*** 1 slot goes to the winner of [[Brawl_Stars_Challengers/Finals|Challengers Finals]]
* '''Group Stage''' - ''October 17<sup>th</sup> - 18<sup>th</sup>, 2026''
** Two Double-Elimination Format (GSL) Groups
** Each group has 4 teams
** All matches are {{Abbr/Bo5xBo3}}
** {{bgcolortext|up|Two teams}} from each group advance to Elimination Matches
* '''Playoffs''' - ''October 18<sup>th</sup>, 2026''
** Double-Elimination bracket
** Two teams (one team from Lower Bracket and one from Upper Bracket) qualify for [[Brawl_Stars_World_Finals/2026|World Finals]]
** All matches are {{Abbr/Bo5xBo3}}
