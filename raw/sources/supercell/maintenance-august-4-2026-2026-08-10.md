## Bug Fixes

- Ability effects are now applied while respawn protection is active.
- Meg's `Heavy Metal` Star Power heals her only if her Super hits an opponent.
- 8-Bit's `Extra Credits` Gadget can no longer shoot over walls.
- Nori can jump over obstacles after his attack is fully charged with his first NanoPower.
- Nori's Super is no longer cancelled when thrown while dashing.
- Nori's dash no longer breaks Gene's Super.
- Text and description fixes.
